Privacy Policy

Crypto & Stocks Dashboard (paper trading simulator) — last updated 2026-08-08

Short version: this app has no backend server and no account system. Nothing you enter or do in the app is ever sent to its developer. Everything — your API keys, your simulated portfolio, your trade history — stays only on your own device.

What this app does

This is an educational dashboard showing live cryptocurrency and stock prices, naive statistical trend signals, and a fake-money ("paper") trading simulator. It does not connect to a real broker, does not move real money, and is not investment advice.

Data we collect

None. The app has no server component, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts. The developer has no way to see what you enter into the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only (in browser localStorage, or its native-app equivalent), and never transmits it anywhere except where noted below:

  • Any third-party API keys you choose to enter (Finnhub, Twelve Data) — used only to make requests directly from your device to that provider.
  • Your simulated ("paper") portfolio: cash balance, holdings, option positions, and trade history.
  • Your price-alert and price-target settings, and your stock watchlist.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data if using the web version, permanently deletes all of this.

Data sent to third parties

To show live prices, news, and charts, the app makes direct requests from your device to these third-party services. No data passes through any server operated by this app's developer:

  • CoinGecko — cryptocurrency prices and history (no key required)
  • Finnhub — stock quotes and news (requires a key you provide)
  • Twelve Data — stock price history (requires a key you provide)
  • CoinStats — crypto news headlines (no key required)

Each of these requests is subject to that provider's own privacy policy, and typically includes standard technical data any web request carries (e.g. your IP address). This app does not control or have access to that data.

Notifications

Optional price-alert notifications are generated entirely on your device from prices it already fetched — no notification service or push server is involved.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be filed as an issue on the GitHub repository.
